Cangnian Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Cangnian handles information related to your use of the App. Cangnian is primarily designed to let you embed text, audio, video, images, or other files into photos on your device and later extract and read that content from those photos.

Last Updated August 14, 2026
Effective Date August 14, 2026
Applies To Cangnian for iOS / iPadOS / Android
Contact 505419863@qq.com

1. Information We Process

Cangnian primarily processes the following types of information locally on your device when you actively use the relevant features:

  • Photos and images: Used to select photos that carry embedded content, read photos that contain embedded content, and save generated photos.
  • Text content: Used to create text-based messages, image-based messages, or related content, and may also be displayed in local history previews.
  • Audio content: When you choose to record or import audio, the App processes that audio so it can be embedded into photos, saved, or played after extraction.
  • Video, image files, and other files: When you actively select a file, the App processes that file so it can be embedded into a photo or extracted from an existing photo.
  • Local history records: The App may store local records of embedding and extraction activity, such as timestamps, file paths, content type, file name, and preview information, so you can review previous activity.
  • In-app purchase and subscription status: When you purchase a membership or subscription, payment and related transactions are handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, or the applicable platform. The App may read purchase status, subscription status, or restore-purchase results returned by the platform in order to determine whether premium features are available to you.
  • Advertising-related information: If the current version of the App displays advertisements, we may use a third-party advertising service. The advertising provider may process device information, advertising identifiers, ad impressions, clicks, interactions, or approximate location information in accordance with its own privacy policy.
Cangnian's core embedding, extraction, and local history functions are primarily performed on your device. Unless you actively use a feature that depends on system or third-party services, we do not intentionally upload the contents of your messages, photos, audio, video, or other files to servers operated by us.

2. Permissions We Use

Cangnian may request the following system permissions in order to provide specific features. These permissions are requested or used only when you actively use the corresponding functionality.

  • Photo library access: Used to select photos, read photos that contain embedded content, and save generated photos to your device's photo library.
  • Microphone access: Used only when you actively use the audio recording feature.
  • File access: Used only when you actively select audio, video, images, or other files through the system file picker.
  • Network access: Used for in-app purchases, subscription status retrieval, advertising services, and other system or third-party services that require an internet connection.

You can disable permissions at any time in your device settings. Some App features may stop working if the required permission is disabled.

3. How Information Is Stored

Cangnian's message embedding, extraction, and history features are primarily handled locally on your device. Generated photos, extracted files, and local history records are generally stored in your device storage, photo library, or another location selected by you.

Unless you actively use sharing, in-app purchases, advertising, or another feature that relies on system or third-party services, Cangnian does not intentionally upload your embedded content to servers operated by us.

4. How Information Is Shared

When you actively use the sharing feature, the App may open the system share sheet and provide the photo, file, or other content you selected to the third-party app, contact, or service that you choose.

Any third-party app, platform, service, or recipient you select will process the shared content according to its own privacy practices. Please make sure the content you share is appropriate for the intended recipient.

5. Third-Party Services

To provide certain features, Cangnian may rely on operating-system platforms or third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Apple App Store: Used for in-app purchases, subscriptions, restoring purchases, and related transaction processing on iOS and iPadOS.
  • Google Play: Used for in-app purchases, subscriptions, restoring purchases, and related transaction processing on Android.
  • System photo library and file picker: Used to select, read, or save photos and files that you actively choose to work with.
  • System sharing services: Used to share content with other apps, contacts, or system services when you actively choose to do so.
  • Advertising services: If the current version displays advertisements, we may integrate a third-party advertising platform. The advertising provider may process device information and information related to ad impressions or ad interactions according to its own policies.

Third-party services collect, use, store, and share information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the platforms or services you use.

6. Children's Privacy

Cangnian is not primarily directed to minors. If you are a minor, please use the App only with the consent and guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

If we become aware that we have processed personal information relating to a minor in circumstances where parental or guardian authorization is legally required, we will take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • You may disable photo library, microphone, and other permissions in your device settings, although some features may no longer function.
  • You may delete supported history records from within the App.
  • You may delete photos and files saved on your device using the system photo library, file manager, or other applicable system tools.
  • You may manage subscriptions, purchases, and renewal status through Apple App Store, Google Play, or the applicable platform.
  • Data stored only within the App is generally removed when the App is uninstalled. Content that has been saved separately to your photo library, Files app, or another location must be deleted separately by you.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information associated with your use of Cangnian. Because embedded content is primarily stored on your device, in photos, in files, or through destinations that you choose to share with, you are also responsible for protecting your device, files, accounts, and recipients.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee that information will be free from all security risks in every circumstance.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in product features, applicable laws, platform requirements, or third-party services.

The updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page, and the “Last Updated” date will be revised accordingly. Where appropriate, we may also provide additional notice within the App or through another reasonable method if a change materially affects your rights.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or privacy-related matters, you can contact us at: 505419863@qq.com.
