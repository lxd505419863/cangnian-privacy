Quick troubleshooting

  1. Update Cangnian to the latest version available on the App Store.
  2. Close and reopen the app, and make sure your device has available storage.
  3. For purchases, restoration, or ads, confirm that your internet connection is working.
  4. For photos or recording, check Cangnian's permissions in the Settings app.

Frequently asked questions

How do I seal a letter inside a photo?

Choose “Seal a letter” in the app, select a carrier photo, add text, audio, video, an image, or a file, then finish the stationery settings and save. You can save the generated photo or share it with someone.

Why can't Cangnian open a letter from a photo?

Use the original photo generated by Cangnian whenever possible. Some social platforms compress, crop, or re-encode images and may damage the embedded data. Try sending the original image as a file.

Why can't I select photos, save photos, or record audio?

Open Settings on your device, select Cangnian, and allow Photos or Microphone access. Cangnian requests these permissions only when you use the corresponding feature.

My membership did not activate after purchase

  1. Make sure the device uses the same Apple ID that made the purchase.
  2. Open Me → Membership, then tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Keep the device online, wait briefly, and reopen the membership page.
  4. If the issue remains, email us the time of the issue, device model, and app version. Never send your Apple ID password or complete payment details.

How do I manage or cancel auto-renewal?

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your Apple ID, choose Subscriptions, then select Cangnian. Subscription management and refunds are handled by Apple.

How do I request a refund?

App Store transactions are handled by Apple. Visit Report a Problem, sign in, and follow Apple's instructions.

When contacting us, please include

  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • The Cangnian app version and build number.
  • Steps leading to the issue, approximate time, and any error message.
  • A screenshot or recording that contains no private letter content or payment information.
For your security, never email Apple ID passwords, payment card information, verification codes, or original files containing private letter content.
